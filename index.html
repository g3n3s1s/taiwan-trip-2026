🐼 Zoo day (Thu 3 Dec): ride up, walk down

  1. 09:00 Enter main gate, walk ~3 min to the tram terminal (by Koala House). Ride to Bird World Station. Fold the stroller before boarding.
  2. 09:20–11:15 Upper zone: Penguin House → Temperate Animal Area → Bird World → Amphibian & Reptile House.
  3. 11:15–12:00 Walk downhill to the African Animal Area.
  4. 12:00–13:00 Lunch and a sit-down break.
  5. 13:00–14:30 Lower zone: Pangolin Dome → Giant Panda House → Koala House → Formosan Animal Area.
  6. 14:30 Exit, MRT back to rest.

Toddler & senior notes

  • Zoo: under 6 free. 65+ free only for ROC nationals or ARC holders, so your 75-year-old pays NT$100 with a foreign passport. Tram NT$5 (under 6 free).
  • Gondola: ages 0–5 free with a paying adult (max 2 per adult). Senior concession is for Taiwan nationals only.
  • Zoo closes 17:00, last entry 16:00, exhibits close 16:30.
