Planned dates: Zoo: Thu 3 Dec (weekday, no Monday indoor-house closures) · Maokong sunset: Mon 7 Dec afternoon. 7 Dec isn't on the gondola's published December stop list (14 and 28 Dec). Recheck the official site the day before. Backup: Taipei 101 observatory at sunset.

🐼 Zoo day (Thu 3 Dec): ride up, walk down

  1. 09:00 Enter main gate, walk ~3 min to the tram terminal (next to Koala House). Ride to Bird World Station. Right after opening is the shortest queue. Fold the stroller before boarding.
  2. 09:20–11:15 Upper zone: Penguin House → Temperate Animal Area → Bird World → Amphibian & Reptile House.
  3. 11:15–12:00 Walk downhill to the African Animal Area (giraffes, hippos, zebras).
  4. 12:00–13:00 Lunch and a proper sit-down at a zoo restaurant or food court.
  5. 13:00–14:30 Lower zone: Pangolin Dome / Asian Rainforest → Giant Panda House → Koala House → Formosan Animal Area (by the gate).
  6. 14:30–14:45 Exit, MRT back to rest. Tired early? Skip the Formosan area. Last tram from Bird World to the terminal is 17:00.

If you still choose Option 1 (split), here's the exhibit split

  • Day A (enter via gondola, Zoo South): tram Zoo South → Bird World (last tram 16:00): Penguin, Temperate, Bird World, Reptile, African. Exit via main gate downhill.
  • Day B (main gate): Koala, Giant Panda, Formosan Animal Area, Children's Zoo, Pangolin Dome / Asian Rainforest, Australian Area.

Toddler & senior notes

  • Zoo entry: under 6 free; tram NT$5 for adults, under 6 free.
  • Gondola: ages 0–5 ride free with a paying adult (max 2 per adult). Concession fares are for Taiwan nationals only, so adults pay NT$180 single; EasyCard caps you at NT$300 per day.
  • Zoo closes 17:00, last entry 16:00, exhibits close 16:30.
